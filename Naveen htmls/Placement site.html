HIRE Score
A composite employability score out of 1000, combining academics, aptitude & communication, technical skills, and industry validation into one number recruiters can compare across students.
Academic & Regulatory150 pts
Xth / XIIth / UG-PG marks (100 pts) plus a clean arrears record — fewer current arrears and no history of arrears scores higher (50 pts).
Cognitive & Linguistic Proficiency300 pts
Aptitude — Quantitative, Logical Reasoning & Verbal Ability (150 pts) — plus Communication — CEFR grammar and EF SET Listening / Speaking / Reading / Writing bands (150 pts).
Technical Proficiency400 pts
LeetCode practice ranking (125 pts), FOP + DSA coding assessments (175 pts), hackathon / codeathon rounds (50 pts), and GitHub mini & full-length projects (50 pts).
Industry Validation150 pts
Recognized global certifications (100 pts) and other relevant certifications (50 pts). Coursera / Udemy / LinkedIn Learning-style certificates are not counted.
Total1000 pts

FPC NQT
FACE Prep Campus National Qualifier Test — a placement-readiness assessment covering Aptitude and Coding, helping students see where they stand and what to improve before campus placements.
Aptitude Score
The student's overall performance across the aptitude sections — Verbal Ability, Numerical Ability, Reasoning Ability, and Advanced Quantitative Ability — reflecting analytical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills. Shown here as the average across whichever of these sections the student has completed.
Coding Score
The student's performance in the coding section of the assessment, measuring programming ability, logical thinking, and problem-solving by evaluating their submitted coding solutions.
Overall
A combined view of Aptitude and Coding performance across all NQT rounds the student has taken, used to track readiness improvement over time.
